Full-precision and compressed calibration inputs yield $H_\ell$ and $\Delta_\ell$; calibration-loss gradients are used to jointly fit $\Phi_\ell$ and $\Psi_\ell$. Stage~1 performs Fisher-guided CEALC truncation to rank $r_1$. Stage~2 fits the remaining output alignment error by ridge regression and activation-eigenspace projection to rank $r_2$. The stages share the budget $r=r_1+r_2$ and form two parallel low-rank branches.}
